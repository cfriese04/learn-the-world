Learn the World

A free, sourced history of the world and of the United States. One idea per page, each with a map or chart. Every claim links to its sources, and practice questions come back when you are about to forget.

1

Read one page

Each page is one idea in a few paragraphs, with a map or chart beside it. Fifteen minutes a day covers a page or two.

2

Check the evidence

Small labels after a sentence say how sure we are: consensus, approx., debated or tradition. On any page, tap one to see the claim and open its sources.

3

Practise, then review

Two questions end each page. Answer them and they return on a schedule that fits your memory; the Review button shows what is due.
